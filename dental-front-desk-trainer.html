Four rules that cover most of it

  • Teeth 1 to 16 are upper. Teeth 17 to 32 are lower.
  • Count starts at the patient's upper right back tooth (1), crosses to the upper left (16), drops to the lower left (17), and crosses to the lower right (32).
  • The midline sits between 8 and 9 on top, and between 24 and 25 on the bottom.
  • The four wisdom teeth are the corners: 1, 16, 17, and 32.

Baby teeth use letters

Primary teeth are A to T. A to J run across the top from the patient's right to left. K to T run across the bottom from the patient's left to right. Example: A is the upper right second primary molar, and T is the lower right second primary molar.
